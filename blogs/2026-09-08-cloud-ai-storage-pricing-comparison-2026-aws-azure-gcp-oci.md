Cloud storage is one of the most deceptively complex line items in any cloud bill. The headline price per gigabyte is only a fraction of what you actually pay. Retrieval fees, egress charges, API operation costs, and replication overhead routinely push real-world spend two to five times higher than raw storage rates.
