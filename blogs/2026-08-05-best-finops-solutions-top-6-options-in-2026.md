What Are FinOps Solutions? FinOps solutions are software platforms and practices designed to manage, allocate, and optimize cloud costs across engineering, finance, and product teams. They turn complex, multi-cloud billing data into actionable insights, covering Kubernetes, AI, and SaaS spend to drive unit economics, automate tag mapping, and provide accurate forecasting.
