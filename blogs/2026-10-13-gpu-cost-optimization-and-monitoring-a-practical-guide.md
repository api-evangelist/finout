Key Takeaways Direct answer: Measure what each GPU does, tie it to cost and owners, then cut idle capacity, rightsize, share, and lower the rate. Why it matters: In GPU cost optimization, a busy GPU and a useful GPU aren't the same thing, and you pay for the gap. Central principle: Fix utilization before you buy commitments, or you lock in waste at a lower rate.
