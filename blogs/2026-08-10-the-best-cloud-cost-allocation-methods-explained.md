Quick Summary Cloud cost allocation assigns every dollar of cloud spend to the team, product, or customer that generated it. The five core methods are resource tagging, account-based allocation, shared cost allocation, virtual tags, and business mapping. Shared costs — resources used by multiple teams — require proportional, fixed, or even-split allocation strategies.
