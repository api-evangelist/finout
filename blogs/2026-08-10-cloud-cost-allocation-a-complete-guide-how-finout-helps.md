Key Takeaways Cloud cost allocation is the process of attributing cloud, Kubernetes, SaaS, and AI spend to the teams, products, or business units that drive it. There are five core allocation types: direct, proportional, even-split, weighted, and virtual (rules-based) allocation. Showback reports costs back to teams; chargeback moves real budget.
