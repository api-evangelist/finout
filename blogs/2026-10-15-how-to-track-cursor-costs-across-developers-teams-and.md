Key Takeaways What it means: To track Cursor costs, you pull usage-level spend, split seat, included, and on-demand cost, and map each developer to an owner. Why it's hard: Two Cursor pricing structures now run side by side, and the model behind each request changes its cost. Where to start: Check your numbers against Cursor's Admin Console usage page, because invoices are cut at billing thresholds instead of calendar days.
