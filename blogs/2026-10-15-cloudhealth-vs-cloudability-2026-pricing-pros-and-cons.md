Key Takeaways Short answer: Both cover visibility, allocation, budgets, forecasting, anomaly detection, and commitment recommendations, but which clouds each feature covers differs by platform. Where CloudHealth stands out: It can bring vSphere data-center costs into the same view. Where Cloudability stands out: It manages commitments across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud from one place, and Kubernetes cost allocation covers OCI too.
