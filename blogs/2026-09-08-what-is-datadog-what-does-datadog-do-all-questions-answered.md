If you're running cloud infrastructure at any real scale, you've almost certainly encountered Datadog. It's a cloud-based monitoring and analytics platform that gives engineering teams real-time visibility into the health and performance of their applications, services, and infrastructure. But Datadog is also a significant cost center in its own right, and increasingly, a source of data that feeds cost allocation and optimization decisions.
