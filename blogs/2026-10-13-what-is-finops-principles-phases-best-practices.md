Key Takeaways What FinOps is: FinOps is an operational framework and cultural practice that maximizes the business value of technology through collaboration between engineering, finance, and business teams. Why it matters now: The practice now covers AI, SaaS, licensing, private cloud, and data center spend, not only public cloud. How it runs: Teams cycle through Inform, Optimize, and Operate continuously, guided by six FinOps Foundation principles.
