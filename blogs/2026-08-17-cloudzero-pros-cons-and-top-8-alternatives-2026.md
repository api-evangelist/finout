What Is CloudZero, and What Are the Best Alternatives? CloudZero is a cloud cost management platform that helps engineering and finance teams track spend and organize it around business metrics like cost per customer, feature, or team. Its main strength is that it can allocate costs even when your resources aren't fully tagged.
